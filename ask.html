Ask a question

You know which questions are worth asking. Tell us one.

The fastest route is three things:

  1. 1
    Input → output. What you have, and what you want predicted from it.
  2. 2
    Data. A link to examples of the input and output: a public link, or a PolyU OneDrive folder shared with jessie-zh.li@polyu.edu.hk.
  3. 3
    Metric. How you would judge a good answer.
